Client: an independent film studio releasing a quiet horror film set in a seaside town out of season.
Audience: film fans on TikTok who are tired of jump-scare trailers.
Goal: make people feel unease in under five seconds and want to know more.
Deliverable: one vertical teaser frame or 5-second clip. No title card needed.
Tone: slow, eerie, beautiful. Dread rather than shock.
